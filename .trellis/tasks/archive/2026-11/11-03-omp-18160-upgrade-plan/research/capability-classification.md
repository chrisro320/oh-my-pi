Rule used for the four KEEP/CONFLICT classes (applied uniformly where slices disagreed, see §6): **MECHANICAL** = hunks apply or
need context-only re-anchoring and the contract is untouched; **SEMANTIC** = contract intact but must be re-expressed on
changed/restructured upstream code, no ownership dispute; **CONFLICT-REDESIGN** = the surface the capability attaches to was
removed, or upstream now owns an overlapping mechanism whose shape contradicts the local design, so the local change must change
shape; **ADOPT-CANDIDATE** = upstream implements the same contract, retire only after the named consumer proof **and** its
negative control pass on pristine 18.16.0.
